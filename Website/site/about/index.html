About

Updated: 8.11.2026

Mystery Quest is fan-made TTRPG ruleset for Mystery Dungeon and other Pokémon-only adventures by u/Finnfox66 (that's me). It is also a conversion kit that allows official mainline video game material to be converted into roleplaying game format. The game is supposed to feel familiar to people who have experience from established TTRPG rulesets like D&D 5th edition.

I have created the ruleset simply so that I could tell a story together with my friends in a custom world that is loosely adapted from the world of Pokémon Mystery Dungeon. I also started this project because game design is something that I am (sometimes a bit too) passionate about so I had specific ideas for what I wanted the ruleset to be like.

As of writing this, the ruleset has not yet been extensively playtested. I have one playtesting campaign ongoing, but the results are currently minimal.

Contacting the author

If you have any feedback or other comments regarding the ruleset, feel very free to reach out to me via Reddit! If you have criticism to give, no need to soften the punches, as criticism is exactly what helps me improve the ruleset further.

Use of AI

AI has never been used nor will ever be used in this project to write final rules text or to produce creative assets. Every single word of the game rules has been written manually. However, modern AI tools have been used to proof-read rules, to provide some initial suggestions for rules, to provide critical analysis of rules, and to write code for this website.

Used AI tools (starting from the most recently used):

  • Mistral Large 4 (Mistral)
  • GLM-5.3 (Mistral hosted)
  • Gemini 3.8 (Google)
